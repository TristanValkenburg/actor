Tristan Valkenburg is a Dutch film actor based in Amsterdam, with prominent roots in theater. He started at a young age, and is currently climbing his way up the industry ladder. Bilingual (English, Dutch), 22 years old.

Growing up, in addition to lesser known companies, he performed with International Theatre Amsterdam, twice. This laid the foundation for the rest of his career.

These days, he works in film - professional and indie sets alike - and continues live performing at his job in The Amsterdam Dungeon. He recently won Best Acting Leading Role at 48HFP Eindhoven 2025 for his performance in his self-written film Kenosis.

He is trained and experienced in his craft. He was accepted into Ma Filmactors Academy, but decided to drop out after a half year to allow himself to prioritize field work instead - no regrets. Acting courses at Mulholland and De Toneelmeester keep his tools sharpened. He is also in physical/creature performance.

Alright, enough professional bio-banter. Feel free to have a look around my website, watch some videos and get in touch if you'd like :)
